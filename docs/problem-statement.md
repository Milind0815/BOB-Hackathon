# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Clinical Operations, Quality, Clinical Data Management and risk-management teams overseeing multi-site clinical trials.

## The problem
When a study has thousands of patient visits across many sites, manual review can miss patterns such as repeated missed visits, incorrect dosing and prohibited co-medications. A risk manager needs a way to move from raw records to a prioritized list of sites that deserve attention.

## Why existing manual review is insufficient
Spreadsheets and periodic review can be slow, inconsistent and reactive. A site may accumulate several leading indicators before the pattern becomes visible in a formal audit.

## TrialGuard response
TrialGuard makes the protocol machine-readable, compares records against the protocol, produces transparent deviation evidence, and turns the findings into site-level risk signals and CAPA-ready drafts.

## Important scope
This hackathon prototype uses synthetic data and does not claim to replace sponsor SOPs, medical review, quality review, source-data verification or regulatory judgment.
