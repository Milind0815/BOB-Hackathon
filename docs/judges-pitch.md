# 60-Second Pitch

Clinical trial risk teams have a visibility problem. When thousands of visits are spread across many sites, missed visits, wrong doses and prohibited co-medications can accumulate before anyone sees the pattern.

We built **TrialGuard AI** — a protocol-aware risk monitor that compares patient visit records with the study protocol, detects deviations, classifies them, ranks sites using leading indicators, and generates CAPA-ready actions.

Our key differentiator is traceability: every site risk signal can be drilled down to the exact patient, visit, rule and evidence that created it.

And IBM Bob is not just in our slide deck. We expose TrialGuard through MCP so Bob can investigate the trial, call the analysis tools and draft an evidence-backed CAPA report.

TrialGuard turns **protocol → evidence → risk → action** into one workflow.
